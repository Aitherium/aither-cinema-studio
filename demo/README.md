# demo/

Scripts that rendered and cut the contest demo: `render_all.py` (shots on Comfy Cloud through the
studio CLI), `cut.py` (beat-synced edit), `qa.py` (motion and contact-sheet checks), `make_demo.py`
(the narrated demo video).

The song "madness." — its lyrics, audio, beat grid and shot list — belongs to the artist. It is
**not** part of this repository and is **not** covered by the MIT licence. All rights reserved.
To use these scripts, bring your own track (`demo/music/<track>.wav` plus a `beatgrid.json`) and
your own `demo/shotlist.json`.
